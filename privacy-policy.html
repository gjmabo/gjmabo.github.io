Privacy Policy

Effective: October 14, 2026. Contact: gjmabo@gmail.com

The short version

Nocturna is a private journaling and wellness app. Everything you write, log, or say to the app stays on your iPhone. Nocturna has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Your data never leaves your device except in two narrow cases, both under your control: downloading the AI model (a one-time download, nothing is sent), and anything you explicitly export or share yourself.

What the app handles (all on-device)

  • Diary entries — text and photos you attach.
  • Mood logs — scores, notes, and activity tags you record.
  • Conversations with Nocturna — the Reflect chat transcripts and rolling summaries, stored locally so you can browse past conversations and so the app can draft journal entries from them.
  • Core memory profile — the optional "what Nocturna knows" fields you fill in yourself.
  • Health data — sleep analysis, step counts, and workouts read from Apple HealthKit with read-only permission, used to show your dashboards and to compute weekly reflections on-device.
  • AI-generated content — entry analyses (emotions, themes), weekly reflections, and journal-entry drafts, all produced on-device.

All of this is stored only in the app's local database on your iPhone. If you enable the app lock, it is additionally protected by your device's biometric authentication (Face ID or passcode) via Apple's LocalAuthentication framework — your biometric data never touches the app.

What leaves the device

  • AI model download (one time). The on-device language model (~2 GB) is downloaded once from Hugging Face's content-delivery servers over the internet. This request contains no personal data — it is a file download. After it completes, the model runs entirely offline.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions (Nocturna Pro) are processed by Apple via StoreKit. Apple handles your payment information; the developer never sees it. The app receives only your subscription status.
  • Anything you export or share. If you use the export feature (Settings → Export My Data) or the iOS share sheet anywhere in the app, you choose what leaves and where it goes. Exported files may contain sensitive personal information — share them carefully.

That is the complete list. There is no account system, no cloud sync, no crash-reporting service, no analytics SDK, and no advertising framework in the app.

On-device AI

All language-model inference — chat replies, entry analysis, weekly reflections, journal drafts — runs locally on your iPhone using Apple's MLX framework. Your writing is never sent to any server for processing.

Location

With your permission ("While Using the App"), Nocturna reads your approximate location only to calculate local sunrise and sunset times for the living-sky background. Coordinates are used in a pure on-device computation and are never stored, logged, or transmitted. If you decline, the app estimates sunrise/sunset from your time zone instead.

Health data (HealthKit)

Nocturna requests read-only access to sleep analysis, step counts, and workouts. This data is used solely to display your Sleep and Wellness dashboards and to pre-aggregate daily summaries on-device for weekly reflections. It is never written back to HealthKit, never transmitted, and never shared with third parties. You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you use voice capture for diary entries, audio is transcribed on-device using Apple's Speech framework. Recordings are processed locally and are not stored or transmitted.

Photos

Photos you attach to diary entries are stored in the app's local storage and displayed only inside the app. They are never uploaded.

Data retention and deletion

Your data lives on your device for as long as you keep the app. You can delete individual diary entries and mood logs inside the app at any time. Deleting the app deletes all of its local data. There is no remote copy to delete because no remote copy exists.

Children

Nocturna is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL and noted in the app. Material changes will be called out explicitly.
